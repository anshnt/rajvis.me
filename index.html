Why sourdough from Rajvi’s Bake House

no. 01 wild starter only

Every bake rises on Rajvi’s sourdough starter — fed daily, slightly spoiled, very alive. Nothing store-bought, no shortcuts: easier to digest, impossible to forget.

no. 02 baked in small batches

This is a home bakery, not a factory. Everything is shaped by hand and baked fresh for your order — which is why orders go in 2 days ahead.

no. 03 everything in jain

The whole menu — breads, bagels, chocolate and chilli oil — is available in Jain. Just say the word when you order.
