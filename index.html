Why sourdough from Rajvi’s Bake House

no. 01 Wild yeast only

Every bake is naturally leavened with a sourdough starter, fed daily and slightly spoiled. Easier to digest, impossible to forget.

no. 02 Baked in small batches

This is a home bakery, not a factory. Everything is shaped by hand and baked fresh for your order, never for a shelf.

no. 03 Worth the wait

Slow fermentation needs a head start, so orders go in 2 days ahead. Good things rise to those who wait.
